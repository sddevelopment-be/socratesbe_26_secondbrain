# About SoCraTes BE

> Join us to learn, practice and discuss software engineering together with
> like-minded, passionate software crafters.

SoCraTes BE is an **unconference**: there is no fixed agenda and there are no
paid speakers. The participants make the programme.

## How a day works

Every day starts with the **marketplace**, an open-space session where anyone
can propose a topic. A proposal can be a talk, a discussion, a hands-on coding
session, a kata, or a question you want help with. Proposals go on the
schedule board, people pick what interests them, and the sessions run.

The open-space principles apply:

- Whoever comes are the right people.
- Whatever happens is the only thing that could have happened.
- When it starts is the right time.
- When it is over, it is over.
- **The law of two feet:** if you are not learning or contributing, go
  somewhere where you are.

## Not a 9-to-5 conference

Conversations continue at dinner, around the fire and well into the night.
Some of the best sessions happen in the hallway. *It is your conference: you
get out of it what you put in.*

## Why a second brain

Sessions are not recorded, and that is deliberate: it keeps the room open and
honest. The cost is that insights fade. This site lets participants write
down what they want to keep, on their own terms. See
[Contribute](../guides/README.md).
