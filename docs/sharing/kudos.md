# Kudos

A pinboard of thanks for what made the unconference work.

## Entries

No entries yet. Be the first.

Kudos thank roles, sessions or groups, never named individuals. Write "the organisers", "whoever ran the Event Storming session" or "the kitchen team". Say what they did and what it made possible. Reviewers remove personal names from entries before they are merged.

```markdown
- **Who** (a role, session or group): one line on what they did and why it mattered.
```

## How to add an entry

- Open a pull request that edits this page and adds your entry under
  "Entries", following the format above. The
  [contribution guides](../guides/README.md) explain how.
- Or post it in the SoCraTes Slack at <https://socratesbe.org/slack/> and an
  organiser will add it for you.
