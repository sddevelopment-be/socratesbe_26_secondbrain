# Recommended books

A pinboard of books worth reading, recommended by people at the unconference.

## Entries

No entries yet. Be the first.

A good entry names the book and its author, and says in one line why it is worth someone's time. Prefer books that came up at, or changed how you think about, the topics of the unconference.

```markdown
<!-- Title, author — one line why — optional link -->
- **Title**, author: one line on why it is worth reading. Optional link.
```

## How to add an entry

- Open a pull request that edits this page and adds your entry under
  "Entries", following the format above. The
  [contribution guides](../guides/README.md) explain how.
- Or post it in the SoCraTes Slack at <https://socratesbe.org/slack/> and an
  organiser will add it for you.
