# Recommended movies

A pinboard of movies and series worth watching, recommended by people at the unconference.

## Entries

No entries yet. Be the first.

A good entry names the title and its director, and says in one line why it is worth watching. Say what it made you think about, and avoid spoilers.

```markdown
- **Title**, director: one line on why it is worth watching. Optional link.
```

## How to add an entry

- Open a pull request that edits this page and adds your entry under
  "Entries", following the format above. The
  [contribution guides](../guides/README.md) explain how.
- Or post it in the SoCraTes Slack at <https://socratesbe.org/slack/> and an
  organiser will add it for you.
