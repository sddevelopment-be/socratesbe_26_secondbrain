# Impressions

A pinboard of anonymous one-line impressions of the unconference as a whole.

## Entries

No entries yet. Be the first.

A good entry is a single honest line about the unconference as a whole: a feeling, a surprise, something you take home. Keep it anonymous, with no names and no attribution. Session-specific notes belong in the [sessions section](../sessions/README.md).

```markdown
- One line, in your own words.
```

## How to add an entry

- Open a pull request that edits this page and adds your entry under
  "Entries", following the format above. The
  [contribution guides](../guides/README.md) explain how.
- Or post it in the SoCraTes Slack at <https://socratesbe.org/slack/> and an
  organiser will add it for you.
